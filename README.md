# Album 77 - Diadem &amp; Crown: Psalms Against the Spoil

Diadem & Crown is a prophetic-worship collection that confronts memetic, mythic, and spiritual afflictions (SCP‑style motifs) and proclaims Christ’s sovereignty, restoration, and protection. Each track names specific contagions of mind, market, and body, then issues psalmic declarations, benedictions, and sealing prayers to contain and heal. The set is designed for focused prayer rooms, prophetic teams, and contexts where careful pastoral framing and cultural sensitivity are provided.

## Tracklists

Song 01 Diadem of Christ — Anthemic protection and healing against memetic and physical contagion.

Song 02 Crown of Wisdom — Prophetic unmasking of riddles and restoration of sight and discernment.

Song 03 Scepter & Coin — Confrontation of economic idolatry and reclamation of just markets.

Song 04 Hallway Voice — Deliverance hymn casting out fear and haunting influence.

Song 05 Sight Restored — Intercessory healing suite for blindness, pain, and community restoration.

Song 06 Veil of Grace — Hymn of mercy to heal fungal, viral, and timebound afflictions.

Song 07 Crown of Peace — Navigational benediction unlocking truth and establishing peace.

#### Prayer
Sovereign Lord, place Your diadem upon our heads and Your crown upon our nations. Where memetic and spiritual evils spread, send Your light to unmask and bind them. Heal the broken, restore markets and minds, and let Your kingdom come until every heart proclaims Your reign. Amen.
